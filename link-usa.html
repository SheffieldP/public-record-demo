Summary

Written by AI from the public record on this page, then read by a person.

Link USA's money comes from corporate sponsors, so the best use of this record is finding the next ones.

  • What the public record shows. A 501(c)(3) since December 2022, under $50,000 a year on paper, 17 corporate sponsors, and no government money or foundation grants on file.
  • Who already funds this cause. 12 insurance-company foundations gave $1,433,840 to LGBTQ+ groups in their 2021 to 2024 tax returns. Only one of them, Gallagher, already sponsors Link USA, through Gallagher Bassett.
  • Where to start. Allstate, based in Northbrook, gave $432,730, the most of any insurer, including $15,000 to Chicago's Center on Halsted. Elevance Health, Liberty Mutual, New York Life, and Cigna come next.
  • One thing to plan for. Link USA's charity status lets it accept foundation grants, which most professional associations cannot. Above $50,000 a year it files a fuller tax return, and its numbers become public.

Who they are

Source: Link USA, About Link.

Link began in London in 2012 as the LGBTQ+ network for the insurance market. It holds the Queen's Award for Voluntary Service and is one of six Inclusion@Lloyd's partner networks.

Link USA launched in December 2021 as a national professional association open to all insurance professionals. Its mission is to make the insurance industry the employer of choice for the LGBTQ+ community.

Chapters: New York City, Chicago, Los Angeles, Atlanta, and San Francisco (launching November 5, 2026).

Who in insurance funds LGBTQ+ work

Source: IRS e-filed returns, Form 990 Schedule I and 990-PF.

Grants that insurance-company foundations reported giving to LGBTQ+ organizations, from their own 2021 to 2024 tax returns. These are the companies already writing checks for this cause.

12 insurance-company foundations reported 132 grants to LGBTQ+ organizations, $1,433,840 in all, in their 2021 to 2024 tax returns. The largest givers: Allstate ($432,730), Elevance Health ($296,804), Liberty Mutual ($225,000), New York Life ($200,000), Cigna ($104,330). Allstate is based in Northbrook and Gallagher in Rolling Meadows; Gallagher's claims company, Gallagher Bassett, already sponsors Link USA. The table lists the 45 grants of $1,000 or more.

What this means for Link USA

Government grants are not the money for a professional association this size. Its money is sponsorship.

The list above is the sponsor pipeline: insurers whose foundations already fund LGBTQ+ causes and are not yet Link USA sponsors.

Above $50,000 a year the IRS requires a fuller return. Plan for the bookkeeping before that line.

As a 509(a)(2) charity, Link USA can take foundation grants. Most professional associations (501(c)(6)) cannot.
