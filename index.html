What is Public Record?

Grants were built for organizations with staff to watch them. Most of the groups doing the hardest work have no one watching.

Public Record reads the public record for you: tax filings, city and state payment ledgers, federal awards, foundation returns, and open notices. It shows you your own picture, tells you what fits, and tells you when something changes.

Built from public records at

  • City of Chicago
  • State of Washington
  • USAspending
  • Internal Revenue Service
  • grants.gov
  • Illinois GATA
  • King County
